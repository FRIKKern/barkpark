# Legendary Paper restart — Experiment plan

Authority: epic `task-a768c69e659add58`, wave `legendary-paper-reader-upgrade-wave-2026-08-06-restart`, wave revision `8a94f6db-1be6-4bbf-ba49-7f3aeed0e737`, inventory digest `227c9defff49f185dc5e580f731ab2419da17fc2dc5cf2304a664e4b230f7ccc`, plan digest `9997fc50db5f1b83f1f53e33bd45dd111b2b06402b07a78b0673d2048f299e45`.

This plan activates only after restart Verify reaches 30/30 typed terminal results and the campaign Paper is byte-equal to live Cycle authority. It requires exactly fifteen new immutable `legendary-experimenter` assignments at medium effort, three at a time. Earlier E01–E03 artifacts belong to an obsolete Cycle wave: new assignments may reproduce or attack them as hypothesis fixtures, but they do not count toward this fleet.

## Frozen problem and gates

The corpus is four revision-pinned published Papers × public, Studio, TUI80, email, and CLI/API. The current readers must retain all 815 canonical blocks, all nonempty authored carriers, 113 authored header cells, 1,374 table body cells, 388 marks where the target supports semantics, and every CCH29 nested-list word. Eleven genuinely headerless tables remain author-intent boundaries; no experiment invents headers. Exact-empty spacers may be removed only with revision-fenced, idempotent, rollback-backed receipts.

Every candidate must cover the verified repair families together:

1. source preservation and PortableDoc dialect compatibility;
2. public/email semantics, MIME truth, responsive containment, and accessible hierarchy;
3. Studio lossless conversion, connected identity, responsive controls, authorization, and reconnect;
4. TUI discovery, two-line active tasks, focus/scroll/click parity, truthful progress/help, history, state recovery, and bounded full-content rendering;
5. distinct document/release/cache/Cycle identities with conditional validators;
6. capability/schema/OpenAPI/help/pagination/source agreement;
7. typed negotiation, perspective, request-ID, error, and terminal-safety behavior;
8. one documented semantic canonicalizer and a separately bounded mutable Related appendix;
9. append-only traceability with no retry-erased failures.

Hard failure threshold is zero for authored-content loss, schema invalidity, inaccessible reading order, silent scope/perspective substitution, false `not_found`, terminal-control leakage, non-idempotence, rollback failure, invented author intent, missing target reader, or corrupted identity domains. Missing browsers, sessions, assistive technology, or mail clients are carried as blocked cells, never proxy-passed.

## Round 5 — Pilot

| ID | Disjoint batch | Capacity evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `restart-experiment-13` | CCH29 across all five readers | Highest nested-list and structure risk; full gate, elapsed time, zero-loss receipts, rollback. |
| `restart-experiment-14` | PDS45 across all five readers | Headerless/null-metadata author-intent cohort; no invented semantics; full gate and rollback. |

Pilot freezes the chosen format, golden fixtures, elapsed time, failure rate and zero-failure threshold, rollback/quarantine rule, and proven batch capacity. Build count is `max(15, ceil(20 / proven_batch_capacity))`; shared foundations require explicit dependency sequencing and never authorize colliding writers.

## Stop condition

Seal only when all fifteen immutable assignments have typed terminal Cycle receipts, one runnable candidate passes every hard gate, three disjoint pilots reproduce stable golden hashes, and safe capacity is measured. Otherwise Experiment is unsuccessful and Build remains blocked.
