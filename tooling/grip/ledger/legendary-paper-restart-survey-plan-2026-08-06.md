The restart runs 20 waves of three exact `epic-surveyor` assignments at medium effort. Each wave owns one immutable Paper-reader unit and answers three different questions. The prior wave is evidence input only; repeating its prompt or terminal payload does not count.

1. **Provenance/current pin:** resolve the current Paper and reader, reproduce the source/block/revision/hash chain, and identify drift or ambiguity.
2. **Live regression/frozen gates:** remeasure the current reader against applicable Round-1 denominators and classify regression, unchanged failure, or improvement.
3. **Negative capability/evidence strength:** run positive, negative, and adversarial controls; classify each material claim as proven, inferred, blocked, or contradicted.

Every report is read-only and includes the direct answer, checked files/tasks/Papers, exact sample sizes, commands or routes, found/not_found/partial results, facts versus inference, contradictions, risks, and unvisited scope. A result is invalid when it merely cites the old report, lacks a current observation, or turns blocked evidence into a pass.
