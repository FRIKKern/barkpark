# Legendary Paper reader repair wave 01 — renewed experiment plan

This is the immutable successor experiment required by predecessor wave `legendary-paper-reader-upgrade-wave-2026-08-06-restart`. That wave completed Survey 60/60, Verify 30/30, and Baseline through Converge 3/3, but every candidate retained at least one hard failure or blocked target reader. Pilot was therefore unauthorized and remains 0/3. The predecessor is evidence, not a template to weaken or overwrite.

The successor opens as a fresh standard Legendary wave. It owns the same logical cohort—four frozen Paper revisions across public, authenticated Studio, TUI80, delivered email, and CLI/API—but freezes a richer inventory record carrying Paper, revision, and reader identity. It must satisfy its own Survey 60, Verify 30, Experiment 15, computed Build at least 15, and Review 15. Prior evidence may focus new work, but does not count toward the new fleet.

## Hard inherited contract

The replacement combines all predecessor Converge contracts:

- E10: unequal aliases quarantine rather than choose precedence; recursive shape validation; long-token containment; write CAS; byte-exact rollback; terminal sanitization; one validated packet through five real reader adapters.
- E11: preserve exactly 113 authored header cells and exactly 11 intentionally headerless tables; assert actual browser client widths; exercise Studio lifecycle, cache freshness, delivered mail, DOM semantics, MIME, and assistive technology through the eight committed fixtures.
- E12: execute all required terminal/platform cells—widths 1/20/40/80/120, typed errors, request IDs, six identities, validators, discovery, pagination, interaction, history, Related, and recovery. A missing, blocked, proxy, or static-only cell is not a pass.

Hard thresholds are zero. Scores never average away a target-reader, preservation, accessibility, geometry, identity, error, mutation, rollback, or control-sanitization failure.

## Survey — 60 fresh attestations

Run three decision-relevant lenses for each of 20 immutable reader units:

1. revision/source/identity and authored-carrier pin;
2. executable real-reader capability plus environment readiness;
3. predecessor-failure reachability and exact repair seam.

Each survey reports found/not-found/partial, commands or captures, sampled denominators, blocked dependencies, and the smallest implementation boundary. Replaying predecessor prose is invalid.

## Verify — 30 load-bearing proofs

Ten waves of three must independently prove or refute:

1. source counts, recursive schema, header intent;
2. alias quarantine, CAS, rollback, idempotence;
3. public DOM, narrow geometry, cache/conditional behavior;
4. authenticated Studio mount, exact viewports, focus/reconnect;
5. delivered MIME and Apple/Gmail/Outlook evidence boundaries;
6. assistive-technology reading order and labels;
7. TUI bounds, sanitization, focus/scroll/click/Enter parity;
8. typed errors, request IDs, identity domains, validators;
9. capabilities/schema/OpenAPI/help/pagination agreement;
10. runnable harness readiness and collision-free implementation seams.

Every blocking environment dependency must become an executable local harness, a named external gate, or an explicit refutation before Diverge.

## Experiment — five rounds of three

### Baseline: repair-contract calibration

- `repair01-experiment-01`: run every E10 preservation/schema/mutation fixture against current production and isolated candidate harnesses; freeze exact failures and denominators.
- `repair01-experiment-02`: run all E11 human-reader fixtures at asserted client widths; distinguish real readers from proxies and freeze availability receipts.
- `repair01-experiment-03`: run the E12 48-cell-per-candidate platform contract plus hostile controls; freeze typed failures, blocks, and request/identity traces.

Round 2 starts only after all three replay twice byte-identically and their combined manifest has no hidden credential material.

### Diverge: three runnable end-to-end candidates

- `repair01-experiment-04`: strict validated canonical packet compiler. Preserve raw source, recursively validate and quarantine ambiguity, then emit revision-fenced reader packets through explicit adapters.
- `repair01-experiment-05`: compatibility AST boundary. Parse legacy and current shapes into a lossless typed AST, preserve author intent, and give every reader the same semantic tree plus reader-specific layout metadata.
- `repair01-experiment-06`: versioned projection service. Preserve source bytes, publish a separately identified canonical projection with validators/provenance, and bind all adapters to that projection.

Each candidate includes executable code, tests, five reader adapters, exact rollback/quarantine receipts, real viewport assertions, terminal sanitization, discovery/identity/error contracts, and two stable reruns. A prose design is invalid.

### Attack: independent hostile fleets

- `repair01-experiment-07`: attack authored preservation, 113/11 table intent, malformed/nested structures, alias conflicts, CAS, retry, rollback, and idempotence.
- `repair01-experiment-08`: attack public/Studio/email/AT semantics, exact 320/390/reflow widths, session expiry/reconnect, MIME clients, and cache freshness.
- `repair01-experiment-09`: attack terminal widths and controls, interaction parity, history/Related/recovery, typed errors, request IDs, six identities, ETags, discovery, and pagination.

A candidate advances only with PASS in every applicable required cell and zero BLOCKED target-reader cells.

### Converge: reproduce one complete winner

- `repair01-experiment-10`: independently rebuild the preservation/schema/mutation core and delete rejected mechanisms.
- `repair01-experiment-11`: independently reproduce real human-reader semantics, geometry, interactions, MIME, AT, and golden frames.
- `repair01-experiment-12`: independently reproduce terminal/platform truth, identities, validators, errors, discovery, recovery, and traceability.

Candidate selection remains forbidden until all three agree that the same runnable candidate clears every hard gate. Otherwise this successor also closes unsuccessful and no Pilot begins.

### Pilot: three disjoint real batches

- `repair01-experiment-13`: CCH29 across all five readers—the nested-list and density stress case.
- `repair01-experiment-14`: PDS45 across all five readers—the headerless/null-metadata intent case.
- `repair01-experiment-15`: CCH28 plus PDS44 across ten reader units—the marks, tones, identity, interaction, and two-Paper capacity case.

Pilot freezes chosen format, golden fixtures, revision, elapsed time, failure rate, zero threshold, rollback/quarantine rule, and proven batch capacity. Build seals only after 15/15 typed terminal results and three reproducible zero-failure batches.

## Stop condition

The wave may enter Build only when one runnable format passes every declared target surface and hard gate, all 15 experiment results are canonical, and the server seals capacity. Otherwise the immutable no-winner record remains truthful and a further wave is required.
