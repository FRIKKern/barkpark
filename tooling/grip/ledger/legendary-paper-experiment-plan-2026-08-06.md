# Legendary Paper reader Experiment plan

Authority: `task-a768c69e659add58` / `legendary-paper-reader-upgrade-wave-2026-08-05` / wave revision `a06716c4-2dd5-4bda-b9a9-a484b009abb2` / inventory digest `3e480a9fcf44da65a07aa1fcad8e981911006568d23b89ad8891f26a5d96e69e`.

Verify is sealed at 30/30. The exact inventory is four pinned Papers × public, Studio, TUI80, email, and CLI/API. All 20 units have mapped candidate repair families, zero are green, and no repair is accepted before this exact 15-assignment Experiment completes. Rounds run in order; each has three independent `legendary-experimenter` assignments at medium effort. Experimenters work only in isolated artifacts and never mutate production or the Campaign Paper.

## Frozen hard gates

A winning format must retain all 815 canonical blocks' authored meaning, all 113 authored header cells, all 1,374 body cells, all 388 mark records where the reader supports semantics, and every Cloud Console 29 nested-list word. Exactly 11 genuinely headerless tables remain deliberate or are diagnosed for author review; no headers are invented. Removing exactly 381 empty spacers is revision-fenced, idempotent, rollback-backed, and never removes non-empty content.

All applicable readers must expose meaningful hierarchy, tables, callouts, landmarks, focus order, revision/provenance, and typed failures. Public, Studio, and email have no page overflow or control collision at 320/390px. Terminal output stays display-cell bounded at 20/40/80/120 while improving hierarchy and content parity. Navigation, history, task relationships, errors, and provenance are observable. A missing real browser, assistive-technology, or mail-client run is blocked/carried, never proxy-PASS.

The failure threshold is zero for authored-content loss, schema invalidity, inaccessible reading order, silent fallback, non-idempotence, rollback failure, missing target reader, or invented author intent.
