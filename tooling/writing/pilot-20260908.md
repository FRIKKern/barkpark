The Chronicle suite has 32 tests. A structural comparison restored the baseline
prompt byte for byte after reversing the approved prose edits. The quiet-period
payload also matched after reversing its sentence and excluding the expected
editorial-digest change in `source_doc`; period identity and Git source digest
were preserved. A locally generated quiet edition rendered through the real

include the required “Register the movement” pointer. TUI evidence includes actual
rendered notices at narrow and normal widths. The email builder was compiled in
memory and rendered directly; subjects, recipients, and caller-supplied URLs were
checked. Temporary rendering harnesses were removed after use.

## Limits and remaining scope

Studio and Cloud source and existing tests were inspected, but their full behavior
suites were not freshly run locally. There was no authenticated Studio browser
capture or mail-client delivery check. The retained decisions preserve existing
text; they do not claim fresh end-to-end validation. Local Go checks used
`CGO_ENABLED=0` because the configured C compiler rejected `-E`.

No cohort item was deferred. Website copy, broader Cloud onboarding, other
documentation and commands, and stored Paper and Chronicle collections remain
uninspected. Vendored code, generated bundles, customer content, and historical
evidence are excluded. Further cleanup belongs in bounded tasks under the parent
mission `task-aa8ea5a92fff0f14`, using this cohort's proposal and verification process.
