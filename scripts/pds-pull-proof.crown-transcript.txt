own headroom floor is TOO LOW BY MEASUREMENT. Gate (b) admits the export at
MemAvailable ≥ 2200 MB, but this export grew the BEAM by ≈ 2235.43 MiB. The floor
is ~35.43 MiB BELOW the demand it is supposed to gate. This run survived because
MemAvailable at fire was 2846 MB — 646 MB of margin the gate does not require —
not because the floor was adequate. A run that passed gate (b) at 2200–2400 MB
would be firing a request it cannot afford, at the live content API.
That is a finding, not a licence: the floor was NOT lowered, and lowering it
remains the most tempting and most dishonest lever available.
