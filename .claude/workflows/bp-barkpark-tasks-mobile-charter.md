## Vision

A phone-shaped hybrid of our chat app and our task app, **Cloud-exclusive at v1**: a plain team member (not an admin) opens the app, logs in via the Cloud device flow, lands in the right dataset without choosing anything they didn't have to (server → workspace → project → dataset cascade, single-option auto-select, last-location memory), and gets three surfaces — **Tasks** (read + fence-free triage), **Chat** (third sibling client of /v1/chat, full floor: send/stream/interrupt/approve-deny), **Papers** (premium cached reader) — as themselves, on a workspace-bound `[read,write,chat]` token no admin handed them. Needs-you push ships in v1 as a **severable** Cloud APNs/FCM relay (slips ⇒ v1.1 without code debt). The product decision is MADE: staged Sweet Spot ("Operator's Leash") on Expo/React Native per the crown paper — inherited here, never re-litigated.
