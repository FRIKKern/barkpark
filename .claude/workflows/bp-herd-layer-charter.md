## Vision

Fleet awareness for Barkpark Chat, tuned for EXTENDED AUTOPILOT. ONE axis: autopilot vs needs-attention. A finished turn is simply `idle` — **`done` does not exist, no read/seen bookkeeping anywhere** (design paper: "deliberately no read receipts").

One first-class `agent_state` column on `chat_sessions` (FOUR states `working|blocked|idle|unknown`; `needs_you`→`blocked`), derived authoritatively by the Recorder at the `publish_activity/2` seam, streamed over ONE fleet SSE (`GET /v1/chat/events`, state-only, epoch:seq replay, fail-closed workspace scope), surfaced in `bp chat`'s herd home + the Studio pill, and driven by agents through MCP session tools. Every surface reads the SAME column — no parallel derivations (epic DoD #2).

Sealed and LIVE: **waves 1–2, grade A−** — s1 (agent_state substrate), s2 (fleet wire), s3 (herd view), s3f (blocked-truth guard), s5 (Dispatcher-native blocked notifications). Wave 3 (this charter) clears the correctness backlog and names the two feature stones left to seal.
