## Vision

Barkpark stores everything — papers, tasks, knowledge — perfectly labeled, so Postgres FTS finds anything fast. AI authors almost all content, so fail-closed is cheap: a rejected publish costs an agent one retry with a machine-readable error. No lenient fallback; excellence is enforced mechanically at the one door that matters: **publish**. North star: *a document is well-labeled iff search finds it. Drafts stay free; publish is the wall.*

Label model: `tags: [{tag, strength 1–100, rationale}]` — bounds 1–12 (hard), norm 2–4 (advisory only), all strengths distinct, **main tag = unique maximum** (derived, never a flag), rationale mandatory per label (the calibration mechanism for the numeric scale).
