The three blind spots, and why two of them are this wave's own doing                                                    
                                                                                                                        
The census has exactly one rot check, at :585-602, and it tests EXISTENCE: a ledger row naming a file that is no longer 
on disk is an error. That catches deletion. It cannot catch the three shapes that actually rot a price column.          
                                                                                                                        
• STALE PRICE. No content hash, no date arithmetic, no re-measurement. pds-record-parity.test.sh will print CPU=4.45s   
  after someone doubles the harness's work, forever, and nothing reds.                                                  
• STALE DISPOSITION EVIDENCE. Five rows cite "by run 2026-08-03: <cmd> rc=N". The census never re-takes any of them. A  
  disposition survives its own repair.                                                                                  
• ORPHANED DISPOSITION. Read the cond at :519-540: legA=yes AND legB=true short-circuits to THROUGH and the ledger is   
  NEVER consulted. A disposition row for a now-THROUGH instrument is silently unread - not an error, not a warning, not 
  printed.                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                        
▌ THE COUPLING THAT MAKES THIS THE SPINE RATHER THAN A TANGENT: two of the three deferred slices the wish orders will   
▌ make the census lie the day they land. pds-w44-hetzner-offline-door puts --selftest-offline through a rider plus      
▌ ELIXIR_TEST_ONLY_PATHS - legA/legB then compute THROUGH and the ENVIRONMENT row at :128 becomes an unflagged orphan   
▌ asserting a refusal that is no longer the disposition. pds-w44-charter-sweep-adjudication takes the sweep from rc=1   
▌ to rc=0 - and CONTENT-RED, evidenced by a run nobody re-takes, outlives the red it names. Price provenance is a       
▌ PREREQUISITE for the wish's own item 2, not an addition to it.                                                        
                                                                                                                        
The direction                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                        
The epic's law has walked one rung per wave. A receipt lies when the value it emits does not descend from the write     
return. A class lies when it does not descend from its substitution (wave 42). A gate lies when its green costs nothing 
to produce (wave 43). Wave 45's sentence is the one the new instrument earns:                                           
                                                                                                                        
▌ A PRICE LIES WHEN IT DOES NOT DESCEND FROM A METER THE INSTRUMENT ITSELF DROVE - AND A DISPOSITION LIES WHEN ITS      
▌ EVIDENCE NAMES A RUN THE INSTRUMENT NEVER RE-TAKES.                                                                   
                                                                                                                        
What the finished wave looks like: ./scripts/pds-door-census.sh still exits 0 on main and still costs seconds - it is a 
door, and its own 3.32 s row is its own gate cost, so any design that prices it out of its own door is disqualified     
before it is written. But every number it prints is now either taken by a meter it drove in THIS run, or a cached       
measurement carrying the content hash of the instrument it prices, so that a changed instrument turns the row PRICE-    
STALE and reds. A --measure verb takes the measurement and emits the exact ledger row, so re-pricing is one command     
rather than a hand ritual - the number descends from a meter the instrument drove, and anyone can re-take it.           
Dispositions whose evidence is a RUN get re-run under an opt-in, threshold-printed tier, so CONTENT-RED cannot survive  
its own repair. And an orphaned ledger row is an ERROR, because that is the exact shape both of this wave's own slices  
create.

The direction debate                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                        
Three directions were developed far enough to cost something, then argued against each other.                           
                                                                                                                        
Rival A - pay the debts, period                                                                                         
                                                                                                                        
Do exactly the wish's items 1 through 5: three deferred slices, two owed reviews, the lv_verdict half, the criterion    
lesson. Do not touch the census - it exits 0, wave 44 declared it done, and reopening it is a fifth wave of arguing     
about a column. This is the disciplined reading of the brief and it has a real claim on the wave.                       
                                                                                                                        
WHY IT LOSES: it hands the wave a false floor. Two of its own three slices silently corrupt the instrument, so "pay the 
debts" manufactures the next wave's finding. And it violates the mandate the user restates every single wave - DERIVE,  
NEVER TRANSCRIBE, INCLUDING FROM THIS BRIEF - by inheriting "the price column is an instrument now" as a premise instead
of running it. I ran it. It is half an instrument, and the half that is prose is the half the column is named after.    
                                                                                                                        
Rival B - measure every price, every run                                                                                
                                                                                                                        
The pure fix: no cache, no ledger, no staleness possible. The census drives the meter for every row on every invocation 
and the price column is a measurement by construction.                                                                  
                                                                                                                        
WHY IT LOSES, on the epic's own evidence: D648 proved wall is not a property of a door on a shared host - a fixed       
workload's wall spanned 5.8x at constant load while its user CPU spanned 27 percent. A re-measuring census becomes the  
most expensive program in its own denominator: 40.33 s for pds-ledger-census_test.sh alone, plus the receipt census     
whose expensive arm D646 showed conceals ~140 s minimum from any outer meter. It would also have to price ITSELF - a    
fixpoint. Worst of all it converts a 4 s door into an ungateable one, killing the census's own THROUGH row, which is the
single most self-defeating outcome available to this wave. The honest design is not "always measure" but "never assert a
measurement you cannot re-take, and know when it expired."                                                              
                                                                                                                        
Rival C - spend the column, gate the cheap doors                                                                        
                                                                                                                        
Take the census output at face value and do the obvious thing the wish's title demands - SPEND IT. Put every affordable 
row through the required gate: hetzner offline at 2.15 s, pds-record-parity.test.sh at 4.45 s, pds-scratch-             
target_test.sh at 8.91 s. Maximum visible progress, and it is exactly what four waves of arguing were FOR.              
                                                                                                                        
WHY IT LOSES AS A WHOLE DIRECTION, and why it is absorbed as a part: gating on unverified prices is D633's sin at one   
remove. pds-scratch-target_test.sh's own shipped row says "Hermeticity on a runner WITHOUT local Postgres is unproven   
here" - gating it is a day-one red on a foreign runner, the identical trap wave 44 documented for sheet-grid-js.yml.    
Rival C survives as exactly ONE new door: the one whose price and green I measured myself today.                        
                                                                                                                        
Why the winner won                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                        
The spine plus the debts, with hetzner-offline as the one new door, is the only direction in which the wish's own item 2
does not manufacture the next wave's finding. It applies the epic's law to the epic's newest artifact, which is what    
every wave of this epic has actually been about. And it converts "spend the column" from a slogan into something that is
safe to do more than once.

threshold-printed, never on --check's default path.
