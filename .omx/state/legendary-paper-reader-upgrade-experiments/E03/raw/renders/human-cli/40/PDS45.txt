The three blind spots, and why two of   
them are this wave's own doing          
                                        
The census has exactly one rot check, at
:585-602, and it tests EXISTENCE: a     
ledger row naming a file that is no     
longer on disk is an error. That catches
deletion. It cannot catch the three     
shapes that actually rot a price column.
                                        
• STALE PRICE. No content hash, no date 
  arithmetic, no re-measurement. pds-   
  record-parity.test.sh will print      
  CPU=4.45s after someone doubles the   
  harness's work, forever, and nothing  
  reds.                                 
• STALE DISPOSITION EVIDENCE. Five rows 
  cite "by run 2026-08-03: <cmd> rc=N". 
  The census never re-takes any of them.
  A disposition survives its own repair.
• ORPHANED DISPOSITION. Read the cond at
  :519-540: legA=yes AND legB=true      
  short-circuits to THROUGH and the     
  ledger is NEVER consulted. A          
  disposition row for a now-THROUGH     
  instrument is silently unread - not an
  error, not a warning, not printed.    
                                        
▌ THE COUPLING THAT MAKES THIS THE      
▌ SPINE RATHER THAN A TANGENT: two of   
▌ the three deferred slices the wish    
▌ orders will make the census lie the   
▌ day they land. pds-w44-hetzner-       
▌ offline-door puts --selftest-offline  
▌ through a rider plus                  
▌ ELIXIR_TEST_ONLY_PATHS - legA/legB    
▌ then compute THROUGH and the          
▌ ENVIRONMENT row at :128 becomes an    
▌ unflagged orphan asserting a refusal  
▌ that is no longer the disposition.    
▌ pds-w44-charter-sweep-adjudication    
▌ takes the sweep from rc=1 to rc=0 -   
▌ and CONTENT-RED, evidenced by a run   
▌ nobody re-takes, outlives the red it  
▌ names. Price provenance is a          
▌ PREREQUISITE for the wish's own item  
▌ 2, not an addition to it.             
                                        
The direction                           
                                        
The epic's law has walked one rung per  
wave. A receipt lies when the value it  
emits does not descend from the write   
return. A class lies when it does not   
descend from its substitution (wave 42).
A gate lies when its green costs nothing
to produce (wave 43). Wave 45's sentence
is the one the new instrument earns:    
                                        
▌ A PRICE LIES WHEN IT DOES NOT         
▌ DESCEND FROM A METER THE INSTRUMENT   
▌ ITSELF DROVE - AND A DISPOSITION      
▌ LIES WHEN ITS EVIDENCE NAMES A RUN    
▌ THE INSTRUMENT NEVER RE-TAKES.        
                                        
What the finished wave looks like:      
./scripts/pds-door-census.sh still exits
0 on main and still costs seconds - it  
is a door, and its own 3.32 s row is its
own gate cost, so any design that prices
it out of its own door is disqualified  
before it is written. But every number  
it prints is now either taken by a meter
it drove in THIS run, or a cached       
measurement carrying the content hash of
the instrument it prices, so that a     
changed instrument turns the row PRICE- 
STALE and reds. A --measure verb takes  
the measurement and emits the exact     
ledger row, so re-pricing is one command
rather than a hand ritual - the number  
descends from a meter the instrument    
drove, and anyone can re-take it.       
Dispositions whose evidence is a RUN get
re-run under an opt-in, threshold-      
printed tier, so CONTENT-RED cannot     
survive its own repair. And an orphaned 
ledger row is an ERROR, because that is 
the exact shape both of this wave's own 
slices create.

The direction debate                    
                                        
Three directions were developed far     
enough to cost something, then argued   
against each other.                     
                                        
Rival A - pay the debts, period         
                                        
Do exactly the wish's items 1 through 5:
three deferred slices, two owed reviews,
the lv_verdict half, the criterion      
lesson. Do not touch the census - it    
exits 0, wave 44 declared it done, and  
reopening it is a fifth wave of arguing 
about a column. This is the disciplined 
reading of the brief and it has a real  
claim on the wave.                      
                                        
WHY IT LOSES: it hands the wave a false 
floor. Two of its own three slices      
silently corrupt the instrument, so "pay
the debts" manufactures the next wave's 
finding. And it violates the mandate the
user restates every single wave -       
DERIVE, NEVER TRANSCRIBE, INCLUDING FROM
THIS BRIEF - by inheriting "the price   
column is an instrument now" as a       
premise instead of running it. I ran it.
It is half an instrument, and the half  
that is prose is the half the column is 
named after.                            
                                        
Rival B - measure every price, every run
                                        
The pure fix: no cache, no ledger, no   
staleness possible. The census drives   
the meter for every row on every        
invocation and the price column is a    
measurement by construction.            
                                        
WHY IT LOSES, on the epic's own         
evidence: D648 proved wall is not a     
property of a door on a shared host - a 
fixed workload's wall spanned 5.8x at   
constant load while its user CPU spanned
27 percent. A re-measuring census       
becomes the most expensive program in   
its own denominator: 40.33 s for pds-   
ledger-census_test.sh alone, plus the   
receipt census whose expensive arm D646 
showed conceals ~140 s minimum from any 
outer meter. It would also have to price
ITSELF - a fixpoint. Worst of all it    
converts a 4 s door into an ungateable  
one, killing the census's own THROUGH   
row, which is the single most self-     
defeating outcome available to this     
wave. The honest design is not "always  
measure" but "never assert a measurement
you cannot re-take, and know when it    
expired."                               
                                        
Rival C - spend the column, gate the    
cheap doors                             
                                        
Take the census output at face value and
do the obvious thing the wish's title   
demands - SPEND IT. Put every affordable
row through the required gate: hetzner  
offline at 2.15 s, pds-record-          
parity.test.sh at 4.45 s, pds-scratch-  
target_test.sh at 8.91 s. Maximum       
visible progress, and it is exactly what
four waves of arguing were FOR.         
                                        
WHY IT LOSES AS A WHOLE DIRECTION, and  
why it is absorbed as a part: gating on 
unverified prices is D633's sin at one  
remove. pds-scratch-target_test.sh's own
shipped row says "Hermeticity on a      
runner WITHOUT local Postgres is        
unproven here" - gating it is a day-one 
red on a foreign runner, the identical  
trap wave 44 documented for sheet-grid- 
js.yml. Rival C survives as exactly ONE 
new door: the one whose price and green 
I measured myself today.                
                                        
Why the winner won                      
                                        
The spine plus the debts, with hetzner- 
offline as the one new door, is the only
direction in which the wish's own item 2
does not manufacture the next wave's    
finding. It applies the epic's law to   
the epic's newest artifact, which is    
what every wave of this epic has        
actually been about. And it converts    
"spend the column" from a slogan into   
something that is safe to do more than  
once.

That attack has real force, and the wave
absorbs it by refusing to argue and     
making it a RUN. The spine ships only if
the verify round can demonstrate, by    
mutation against a real tree, a case    
where the shipped census prints a number
or a class the tree contradicts. I      
already hold one candidate - the hetzner
row's wrong-arm evidence - and this     
wave's own slices manufacture two more. 
IF THE VERIFIERS CANNOT MAKE THE SHIPPED
CENSUS PRINT A FALSEHOOD AGAINST A REAL 
TREE, THE SPINE IS CUT AND THIS WAVE    
BECOMES RIVAL A. That is the fail-before
condition, declared before the fleet    
flies, and it is the honest version of  
"derive, never transcribe" turned on my 
own direction.                          
                                        
Second-order absorption, as a criterion 
rather than a hope: the design must not 
balloon the census's own price. A       
content hash per row is one shasum      
across 20 files - milliseconds. Re-     
running cheap dispositions is opt-in and
threshold-printed, never on --check's   
default path.

PDS-D664 — A REPAIRED PREDICATE CARRIES 
ITS OLD DEFECT FOR EXACTLY ONE LINE.    
arg_span replaced the three-line        
proximity window with a real paren-     
balance walk, and the three fraud shapes
flipped as designed. It still returned  
the whole CLOSING LINE, so              
"System.cmd(...) ;                      
File.regular?(@fraud)" — a token named  
after the closing paren, on the call's  
own line — still read as argument-list  
membership: bound, never executed,      
THROUGH with a price. One line of the   
old window living inside the new        
predicate, past a 13-arm green selftest.
THE RULE: when you replace a window with
a boundary, the boundary is where the   
window's last residue hides — test the  
token that sits just OUTSIDE the new    
edge, not only the ones that sat inside 
the old one.                            
                                        
PDS-D665 — BOTH LEGS OR NEITHER APPLIES 
TO EVIDENCE STRINGS, NOT ONLY TO        
DECLARED PATH SETS. The wish states the 
both-legs law for the hetzner slice's   
ELIXIR_TEST_ONLY_PATHS entry. It bit    
somewhere nobody named: the sweep's own 
fix makes --selftest rc=0, which turns  
the door census row whose evidence      
quotes "--selftest rc=1" into a stale   
receipt the moment it merges — on the   
instrument whose entire subject is      
receipts that lie. The builder was      
fenced out of the census and correctly  
recorded the correction in two durable  
venues rather than reaching across the  
fence. Review co-landed the one-line row
edit on the SAME branch: a behaviour and
the receipt describing it merge together
or not at all. A fence that splits those
two has to be closed by whoever holds   
both, and the fix belongs on the branch 
that causes the drift.
