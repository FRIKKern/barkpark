The three blind
spots, and why two
of them are this
wave's own doing

The census has
exactly one rot
check, at :585-602,
and it tests
EXISTENCE: a ledger
row naming a file
that is no longer on
disk is an error.
That catches
deletion. It cannot
catch the three
shapes that actually
rot a price column.

• STALE PRICE. No
  content hash, no
  date arithmetic,
  no re-measurement.
  pds-record-
  parity.test.sh
  will print
  CPU=4.45s after
  someone doubles
  the harness's
  work, forever, and
  nothing reds.
• STALE DISPOSITION
  EVIDENCE. Five
  rows cite "by run
  2026-08-03: <cmd>
  rc=N". The census
  never re-takes any
  of them. A
  disposition
  survives its own
  repair.
• ORPHANED
  DISPOSITION. Read
  the cond at :519-
  540: legA=yes AND
  legB=true short-
  circuits to
  THROUGH and the
  ledger is NEVER
  consulted. A
  disposition row
  for a now-THROUGH
  instrument is
  silently unread -
  not an error, not
  a warning, not
  printed.

The direction debate

Three directions
were developed far
enough to cost
something, then
argued against each
other.

Rival A - pay the
debts, period

Do exactly the
wish's items 1
through 5: three
deferred slices, two
owed reviews, the
lv_verdict half, the
criterion lesson. Do
not touch the census
-
it exits 0, wave 44
declared it done,
and reopening it is
a fifth wave of
arguing about a
column. This is the
disciplined reading
of the brief and it
has a real claim on
the wave.

WHY IT LOSES: it
hands the wave a
false floor. Two of
its own three slices
silently corrupt the
instrument, so "pay
the debts"
manufactures the
next wave's finding.
And it violates the
mandate the user
restates every
single wave -
DERIVE, NEVER
TRANSCRIBE,
INCLUDING FROM THIS
BRIEF - by
inheriting "the
price column is an
instrument now" as a
premise instead of
running it. I ran
it. It is half an
instrument, and the
half that is prose
is the half the
column is named
after.

Rival B - measure
every price, every
run

The pure fix: no
cache, no ledger, no
staleness possible.
The census drives
the meter for every
row on every
invocation and the
price column is a
measurement by
construction.

WHY IT LOSES, on the
epic's own evidence:
D648 proved wall is
not a property of a
door on a shared
host - a fixed
workload's wall
spanned 5.8x at
constant load while
its user CPU spanned
27 percent. A re-
measuring census
becomes the most
expensive program in
its own denominator:
40.33 s for pds-
ledger-
census_test.sh
alone, plus the
receipt census whose
expensive arm D646
showed conceals ~140
s minimum from any
outer meter. It
would also have to
price ITSELF - a
fixpoint. Worst of
all it converts a 4
s door into an
ungateable one,
killing the census's
own THROUGH row,
which is the single
most self-defeating
outcome available to
this wave. The
honest design is not
"always measure" but
"never assert a
measurement you
cannot re-take, and
know when it
expired."

Rival C - spend the
column, gate the
cheap doors

Take the census
output at face value
and do the obvious
thing the wish's
title demands -
SPEND IT. Put every
affordable row
through the required
gate: hetzner
offline at 2.15 s,
pds-record-
parity.test.sh at
4.45 s, pds-scratch-
target_test.sh at
8.91 s. Maximum
visible progress,
and it is exactly
what four waves of
arguing were FOR.

WHY IT LOSES AS A
WHOLE DIRECTION, and
why it is absorbed
as a part: gating on
unverified prices is
D633's sin at one
remove. pds-scratch-
target_test.sh's own
shipped row says
"Hermeticity on a
runner WITHOUT local
Postgres is unproven
here" - gating it is
a day-one red on a
foreign runner, the
identical trap wave
44 documented for
sheet-grid-js.yml.
Rival C survives as
exactly ONE new
door: the one whose
price and green I
measured myself
today.

Why the winner won

The spine plus the
debts, with hetzner-
offline as the one
new door, is the
only direction in
which the wish's own
item 2 does not
manufacture the next
wave's finding. It
applies the epic's
law to the epic's
newest artifact,
which is what every
wave of this epic
has actually been
about. And it
converts "spend the
column" from a
slogan into
something that is
safe to do more than
once.

The sharpest attack
on the winner, and
how the wave absorbs
it

THE ATTACK: "You are
reopening a slice
that shipped 11/12
with run evidence
and rc=0 on main, on
a distinction -
'measured' versus
'cached with
provenance' - that

defect. Nobody is
harmed by a three-
day-old CPU figure.
This is the fifth
consecutive wave
arguing about a
column, which is
precisely what wave
44 ended."

That attack has real
force, and the wave
absorbs it by
refusing to argue
and making it a RUN.
The spine ships only
if the verify round
can demonstrate, by
mutation against a
real tree, a case
where the shipped
census prints a
number or a class
the tree
contradicts. I
already hold one
candidate - the
hetzner row's wrong-
arm evidence - and
this wave's own
slices manufacture
two more. IF THE
VERIFIERS CANNOT
MAKE THE SHIPPED
CENSUS PRINT A
FALSEHOOD AGAINST A
REAL TREE, THE SPINE
IS CUT AND THIS WAVE
BECOMES RIVAL A.
That is the fail-
before condition,
declared before the
fleet flies, and it
is the honest
version of "derive,
never transcribe"
turned on my own
direction.

Second-order
absorption, as a
criterion rather

balloon the census's

shasum across 20

milliseconds. Re-

dispositions is opt-

path.
