Direction debate -
the rivals, and why
this one won

Rival A - RECOVERY
ONLY

Spend the whole wave
making wave 43's
four branches
independently
bulletproof and
merged, plus the
charter, and ship
nothing new. The
case for it is
strong and honest:
an unreviewed
authorization change
is the single
riskiest object in
the repo right now,
and a wave that
merges it carelessly
while chasing new
capability has
failed at the only
thing that mattered.
It was rejected
because it under-
uses the wave, not
because it is wrong:
four PRs of
independent re-
derivation is
roughly one round,
and the fold plus
the ladder are
DESIGNED work with
their obligations
already derived by
run - leaving them
unbuilt for a second
consecutive wave is
how wave 43's best
output rots. Rival
A's real
contribution
survives: movement 1
goes first, alone,
and no slice PR
merges on its own
author's oracle.

Rival B - ADVANCE
ONLY

Treat the four
branches as
inherited fact,
merge them on their
green, and spend the
wave taking the door
from 3 of 19 to 6 or
7. Rejected
outright: it is
precisely the move
the wish forbids,
and the epic's own
law forbids it too.
A commit message is
an exit code with
better prose.
Merging #9332 on its
own test's word is
'a gate lies when
its green costs
nothing to produce',
committed by the
wave that coined the
sentence.

Rival C - THE DOOR
CENSUS (winner, as
movement 3 of three)

The winning insight
is that the epic has
spent four waves
arguing about a
column it has never
computed. Every wave
restates the roster,
re-prices some rows,
and writes the total
into the charter by
hand - and PDS-D636
records that the
census itself
carries a FALSE
SENTENCE about its
own gating on main
today. That is the
exact failure mode
this epic exists to
end, committed by
the epic, about the
epic. Making the
column an instrument
is the epic's own
law turned inward
one more notch, and
it is cheap: the
derivation is two
greps over files
that already exist,
and the prior art is
already on main
(api/test/barkpark/p
ds_residue_lens_test
.exs).

The sharpest attack
on the winner, and
how the wave absorbs
it

THE ATTACK: the door
census is a THIRD
census. This epic
already has a
receipt census, a
residue lens, a
ledger census, a
record-parity arm
and a charter-ledger
sweep - nineteen
instruments, three
of which run
anywhere. Building a
twentieth instrument
to count the
nineteen is the
purest possible
instance of an epic
measuring itself
instead of shipping.
And it will be WRONG
on day one: leg A
('a test file shells
it') has at least
three encodings in
the repo already
(System.cmd with a
module attribute,
Code.require_file, a
bare relative path),
so a naive
derivation will
under-count and
print a number LOWER
than the truth - a
different lie, in a
friendlier
direction.

HOW THE WAVE ABSORBS
IT. First, the door
census is movement
THREE of three - it
ships only if
movements 1 and 2
are honest, and the
wave is graded on
the security fix and
the merges
regardless. Second,
the under-count
objection becomes
the instrument's
design: the census
is FAIL-CLOSED on
ambiguity - a pds-*
script it cannot
dispose is
UNDISPOSED and reds,
and a test-side
reference it cannot
classify is an
ERROR, never a
silent 'not gated'.
That makes the day-
one wrongness LOUD
instead of quiet,
and the surveyors
are tasked (question

every encoding of
leg A that exists on
main before a
builder writes a
regex. Third, if the
survey shows the
derivation cannot be
made honest cheaply,
the fallback is
smaller and still
real: a self-check
that the two legs
AGREE for every
already-gated
instrument - which
alone would have
caught #9290 and
#9292.
