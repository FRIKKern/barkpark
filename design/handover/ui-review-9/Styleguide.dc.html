04 · Buttons & states

Hover: primary lightens to --mint-hover; ghost/danger brighten border only. Danger always ends in "…" (a confirm follows, never immediate). Destructive confirms are type-the-name; the button stays visibly gated until the name matches.

Pill grammar: glyph + colour + word — three redundant channels, told ONCE per row. ● live/healthy · ▲ degraded/warning · ◐ in-flight · ■ halted. Provider marks are hollow (identity, never status). The plan chip is the only place plan state appears outside Billing.
