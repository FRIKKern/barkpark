# Barkpark · Monday 23 November 2026

11 pull requests merged. Six went to BPML, the text form of a Paper that `bp paper pull` prints and `bp paper push` sends back: four to nested lists, two to the history a push leaves. One went to the desk, Barkpark's own document editor, one to the reader of legacy Papers, and three to BarkUI.

## What a person can now do

**Pull and push a nested list** ([#28384](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28384)).

1. `bp paper pull <slug>` on a Paper with a nested list. It prints as nested `<ul>` lines: every child list, each item's `id`, a `checked="true"` item and each item's words.
2. Edit a word at any level and `bp paper push <slug>`. Every level and every item id is stored.
3. A copy pulled before nested lists printed cannot overwrite them: such a push is refused, naming the list, with the hint `update bp, then bp paper pull and make your edit again`. Editing nested lists from a text copy needs a `bp` that sends the pull's fingerprint.
4. Two items with one id in a block are refused by that id. Lists nest as deep as a stored Paper may; one level more is refused as `too-deep`.

**Older lists and hidden parts** ([#28438](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28438), [#28488](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28488), [#28464](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28464)). A list stored in an older shape (a bare-string item, words only in `text`, an alias list type) now takes an edit. An item with an id keeps through the edit the parts BPML does not print, such as an inactive `text` or an extra key; deleting such an item is refused, naming its paths. A pull answers `x-bpml-carried-count`, and `?format=carried` names each item a push will carry back, so a client can say which ones before the push.

**A push leaves two revisions** ([#28414](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28414)). An accepted push now stores the Paper as it stood before (`bpml-push`) and as the push left it (`bpml-push-after`), in the same transaction as the write. A refused or empty push stores none, and a row that fails to store rolls the write back with a 500. Both rows show in the history and can be restored.

**What a Paper lost, from its history** ([#28398](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28398)). `Papers.BodyLosses.report/3` reads one Paper's revisions and lists the list item children and callout bodies that disappeared between stored states, naming each step a BPML push or not. It writes nothing, and has no route or command yet.

**Leaving the desk asks first** ([#28408](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28408)). Hold a field unsaved, because its save was refused or kept over another editor's rewrite, then click another row, a breadcrumb, a pane's ×, Back, Create new or a search hit.

1. A dialog asks "Leave this document?" with "Not saved: Title. What you typed there will be lost." (nb: «Forlate dokumentet?»).
2. **Stay** keeps the document and the text, and puts the caret back where it was.
3. **Leave** goes where you asked. Before, all of these dropped the text without asking. A tab close or reload now asks over a clash hold too.

**Legacy Paper HTML, cleaned by a parser** ([#28404](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28404)). A legacy Paper's `body_html` is now read with an HTML5 parser before it is shown in the reader, the Studio's read-only view and a share link. Only allowed attributes stay, so an `onerror` written as `<img/onerror=…>` or right after a quote is gone; before, those spellings came through, and the Studio's read-only view has no content security policy behind it. A `javascript:`, `vbscript:`, `blob:` or non-image `data:` address becomes `#`.

## BarkUI

Told in [its own README](../../js/packages/barkui/README.md): the check reads a look only on a page at rest ([#28389](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28389)), and puts the scroll back after each capture ([#28515](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28515), [#28527](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28527)).

## Where it is slow or rough

- An older `bp` without the pull's fingerprint cannot edit nested lists at all: a list with nested lists or item ids must come back byte-equal, or the push is refused ([#28384](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28384), [#28438](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28438)).
- A push from before #28414 has only its before revision; the loss report prints that step as "after not recorded" and never guesses ([#28414](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28414), [#28398](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28398)).
- Search text does not read an item's `text` the reader does not show, or nested items shown by their `text` ([#28464](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28464)).
- The cleaner now runs on every legacy read with a runtime parser that was test-only; caching it by body is left for when those reads are hot ([#28404](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28404)).

## Across the projects

BPML is how agents and people edit Papers from a terminal. Barkdown, which keeps its notes in Barkpark, changed how far its assistants may go: its default approval mode now lets an assistant change settings, profiles and deletions, with Ask first to keep them for the person ([FRIKKern/barkdown#177](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkdown/pull/177)). The Studio merged nothing on Monday.
