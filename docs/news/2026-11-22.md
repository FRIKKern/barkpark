# Barkpark · Sunday 22 November 2026

43 pull requests merged. Thirteen went to two people editing one document at once in the desk, Barkpark's own document editor, being rebuilt to match the TanStack Studio (the separate Studio app). Eight more went to the desk and schemas, four to rich text and links, four to Papers and BPML (the text form `bp paper pull` prints), three to the readers, two to read-only fields, one to history restore, one to test tooling and six to BarkUI. One was Saturday's News page ([#28306](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28306)).

## What a person can now do

**Edit while someone else saves.** Open one document in two tabs, A and B.
1. In A, change the excerpt and leave it unsaved; in B, save a new title. A takes B's title, keeps its excerpt, and shows no conflict ([#28176](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28176)). Before, any save by B raised "Updated by another user".
2. Change the same field in both. A's text is kept and the save region says, in TanStack's words: "Someone else rewrote Title meanwhile. Your text is kept here, not saved: type on to save yours over theirs." ([#28302](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28302)).
3. Words A typed next to a sentence B cleared are a clash too, no longer written back over B's clear ([#28256](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28256)). A choice A sets back to its earlier value after B changed it is saved, not dropped ([#28275](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28275)).
4. Inside an object, a save of `meta.code` keeps B's newer `meta.label` ([#28312](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28312)), and two people typing in `meta.label` keep both people's words ([#28331](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28331)). Rich text inside an object takes B's save in place, and a post A typed over it is held with a banner ([#28349](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28349), [#28344](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28344)).
5. After B renames, A's pane header and browser tab show the new title ([#28322](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28322)); after B discards the draft, they show the published title ([#28361](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28361), [#28343](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28343)). While A holds a title unsaved, A's list row shows it ([#28334](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28334)), and each keystroke re-sends only that row: 10 keystrokes on a 100-row list cost 230,865 bytes, down from 398,235 ([#28363](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28363)).

**Rich text keeps more.** Indent a list item, outdent it or make it numbered without changing its words, and the change is now stored ([#28354](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28354)). An edited block keeps its other stored fields and a custom `listItem` such as `check`; the review of #28354 had measured 645 of 2,000 changed items losing a field ([#28360](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28360)). A stored `javascript:`, `data:` or `vbscript:` link is drawn as its words in the editor ([#28371](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28371)), and a write that brings one into rich text gets 422 `pt_link_href_refused` ([#28393](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28393)).

**Delete and arrays in the desk.** After a delete, the focus lands on the row next to the deleted one, not one past it; "Disconnect references and delete" says how many references it removed; the delete and unpublish dialogs draw at most 100 referrers, with "Showing the first 100 of 120." ([#28243](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28243), [#28321](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28321)). An array of `text` with `options.layout: "tags"` draws as tags, and a `string` array with `options.list` as checkboxes, as in TanStack ([#28246](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28246), [#28258](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28258), [#28292](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28292)). An `arrayOf` whose `of` names no type shows read-only instead of crashing the editor, and every schema write now refuses one, naming its path ([#28296](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28296), [#28304](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28304), [#28314](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28314)).

**Locked once created.** A readOnly condition that names `_createdAt`, `_updatedAt`, `_id` or `_type` reads the stored row. So a "locked once the document exists" rule (`{"field": "_createdAt", "operator": "non_empty"}`) now refuses a non-admin patch on a saved document with 422 ([#28205](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28205), [#28266](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28266)).

**Papers and history.**
- `bp paper push` may no longer change what `bp paper pull` did not show. Editing a block with parts BPML does not print, such as a list item's child blocks, answers 422 `bpml_unshown`, and a push whose pull no longer matches the stored view answers 412 ([#27556](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/27556), [#28366](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28366), [#28368](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28368)).
- A push that writes records the Paper as it stood before as a `bpml-push` revision; the history panel lists it as "Edited", and it can be restored ([#28381](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28381)).
- Restore in the Studio's history sends the state the page showed. Pressed after someone else wrote the document, it is refused with "The document changed since you loaded it — reload and retry." and the page shows the newer words ([#28096](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28096)).

**Readers.** The five readers keep a word under deep nested inline and write "Nested too deep to show" after it, where before they dropped the word ([#28170](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28170)). A list item one block past the depth limit keeps the notice ([#28323](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28323)). `toPlainText` in `@barkpark/react` reads inline text the way the code chip does ([#28257](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28257)). A test file a js-tests step runs by name now runs only there ([#28272](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28272)).

## BarkUI

Told in [its own README](../../js/packages/barkui/README.md): the check reads popovers and dialogs open ([#28122](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28122)); each selftest case runs under one 60 s limit ([#28277](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28277)).

## Where it is slow or rough

- A held unsaved text over 20,000 units, or a held change over 700 units, shows the conflict banner on every remote save, even a one-word one far away. It keeps A's text; it costs a banner TanStack would not show ([#28256](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28256)).
- In the old Studio, and with two posts in flight, a stale value of another top-level field can still be written; a list row is still written whole ([#28312](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28312)).
- A `bp` from before #27556 sends no view fingerprint, so a field made public between its pull and push is written over. Of 109 pullable blocks in the test corpus, 25 are frozen for BPML edits ([#27556](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/27556)).
- A workspace-bundle import writes by raw `COPY` and does not yet refuse a `javascript:` link ([#28393](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28393)).
- A restore in paper view writes the Paper's draft while the view shows the published row, as before ([#28096](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28096)).
- An `empty _createdAt` field is locked on the server while the Studio shows it editable on a saved document ([#28205](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28205)).

## Across the projects

Most of the two-editor work copies the TanStack Studio's own rules, its `rebase` and `merge3`, file by file. Portable Doc synced both reader changes the same day ([FRIKKern/portable-doc#150](https://github.com/FRIKKern/portable-doc/pull/150), [#153](https://github.com/FRIKKern/portable-doc/pull/153)).
