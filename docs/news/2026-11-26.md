# Barkpark · Thursday 26 November 2026

53 pull requests merged. 19 went to two people editing one document, in the desk, Barkpark's document editor, and in the Studio. 12 went to the leave question, the dialog that asks before held words are dropped. 7 went to who may create a workspace, 9 to public papers and the cache that names the Default workspace, and one dropped twelve translation strings nothing used ([#29005](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29005)). Four went to BarkUI, and one was the News for Wednesday.

A held field is one under the conflict banner: another editor's save is stored, and your words wait in the form.

## What a person can now do

**The Studio asks before Back drops held words** ([#28937](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28937), [#28953](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28953)). On Wednesday the Studio's browser Back did not ask yet.

1. Open a document in two tabs, A and B. Let B save while A types, so A's field is held.
2. In A, press browser Back. The desk's leave dialog opens and names the field. Stay keeps A's words; Leave goes.
3. A list row, a crumb or New document asks with the same dialog, not a flash. So does a link to another page of the site: `window.confirm` is gone. A link to another site is left to the tab's own leave prompt.
4. Back, then Forward to the held document, counts as Stay: the words stay held ([#28967](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28967)).

Checked in headless Chrome, in en and nb_NO, light and dark.

**Nothing lands in the wrong project under the question** ([#28968](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28968), [#28976](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28976), [#28982](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28982), [#29001](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29001)). After a Back into another project of the workspace, the page's project had moved and the held document had not. A save then wrote a copy of it into the other project. Now every write is refused with "Not saved: the page has moved to another project. Choose Stay or Leave first." A reload, a sort and View graph are held with their own notice, a paper's live frames wait, and the notices read in Norwegian under nb_NO ([#28990](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28990)). On the desk, another project's discard of a draft with the same id no longer reloads the open editor and drops its held words ([#28981](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28981)).

The dialog's Leave also refuses a link a browser could read as another site, loads a path the page does not route as a page, and on the desk checks the press's permissions again ([#28959](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28959), [#28963](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28963), [#28966](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28966)).

**Two editors: more saves are merged or held, not written over.**

- A top-level rich text is now read under the document's lock ([#28933](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28933)). On Wednesday the same word stored A's with no banner, and different words lost B's.
- Array rows go by their key ([#29036](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29036)). When B deleted row r1 while A's post was on its way, A's post undid the delete and r2 was gone. Now each row's fields go to the row with its key, and a row that is gone is dropped. Three more fixes carry that to other row fields ([#29044](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29044), [#29047](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29047), [#29040](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29040)).
- A page that loses its connection and rejoins keeps its typing. On the desk, what A typed offline in the focused field was lost; now it stays ([#28973](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28973)). In the Studio, when B saved a string field meanwhile, A's next key wrote over B's save and the banner went. Now the field stays held until Save or Reload, and the banner says "Autosave is paused for … until you Save or Reload." ([#29043](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29043)). An array op, a tag press, a tree pick or a slug Generate on another field no longer writes held words over B's save or clears the banner ([#29050](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29050)).
- The merge got finer: an edit both sides made beside a block one side added now merges, links keep their own keys, B's selection stays as B made it, and a page without the rich text sync script is read against what it was drawn ([#28928](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28928), [#28927](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28927), [#28923](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28923), [#28896](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28896)).
- Cost, in reductions, the Erlang VM's unit of work: 9,998 pasted blocks sharing one key now merge in 3.8 million, down from 611.8 million ([#28929](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28929)). Past the 15,000-object cap, each compare of a 50,000-object value falls from about 26.5 million to under 5 million ([#28954](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28954)).

**Creating a workspace has one rule** ([#28980](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28980), [#28999](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28999), [#29014](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29014)). REST, the Studio and the chrome (the frame around Media, the desk and other non-Studio pages) now ask the same question:

1. The caller needs a write seat in the workspace it acts from: on REST, the token's home. A new user's first token still needs only write. `POST /api/workspaces` now answers 403 where it answered 201 for a write token with no write seat at home, or a user's token whose owner has none.
2. That workspace, or the token's home, must not be suspended. Before, a suspended tenant's token got a 201, and the Studio said "Workspace created". Now REST answers 403 `workspace_suspended` with the operator's reason in `details.reason` ([#29010](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29010)), and the Studio and the chrome say "This workspace is suspended, so no workspace can be created from it", in Norwegian under nb_NO ([#29021](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29021)).
3. On the desk, a token cut to read mid-session creates no project or workspace ([#28974](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28974)). In the Studio, a read token behind a writing account is refused, where it used to become the new workspace's owner ([#28994](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28994)).

**Same-slug papers in two projects** ([#28987](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28987), [#28998](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28998), [#29008](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29008)). Two published papers with one slug in two projects of the Default workspace made `/papers/<slug>` a 500, and which one a reader got followed the database's row order. Now the oldest is served. A write to one no longer repaints the other's open editor or reader, and deleting the open paper reads as gone, not as its sibling. The revision header and ETag now name the paper the body shows; before, a 304 could bring back a body never served ([#29009](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29009), [#29028](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29028), [#29033](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29033)). The cache that names the Default workspace no longer keeps an old one after a bundle import or a load that straddles a change ([#29030](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29030), [#29048](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29048)).

## Where it is slow or rough

- On a rejoin, a top-level rich text still writes over a save B made while A was away, with no banner ([#28933](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28933)). A field that is not focused takes the server's value, so offline typing there is lost, on the desk and in the Studio ([#28973](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28973)).
- If B moves or deletes A's row and also edits it while A's post is on its way, B's edit is lost, as before ([#29040](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29040)).
- After one paper is unpublished and published again, its same-slug sibling holds `/papers/<slug>` for good, and an open reader and a fresh load can disagree; that waits on a rule decision ([#28998](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28998), [#29008](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29008)).
- On main before #28954's change, a 50,000-object autosave cost 318.5 million reductions, most of it in the parse, the draw, the diff and the write ([#28954](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28954)).
- Most of these PRs were not run in a browser; four were checked in headless Chrome ([#28937](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28937), [#28953](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28953), [#28973](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/28973), [#29043](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/29043)).
